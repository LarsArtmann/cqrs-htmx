## Overview

The AuditLog subscribes to the full user lifecycle plus the auth
audit journal (allAuditedEventTypes). Tenant, membership and bot
events are NOT audited.

## Relationships

- ListensFrom **UserRegistration** with events `UserRegistered`, `UserDeleted`
- ListensFrom **UserProfile** with events `EmailChanged`, `DisplayNameChanged`, `EmailVerified`, `TOTPEnabled`, `TOTPDisabled`, `CredentialAdded`, `CredentialRemoved`, `CredentialUsed`, `ExternalAccountLinked`, `ExternalAccountUnlinked`
- ListensFrom **AuthAudit** with events `LoginSucceeded`, `LoginFailed`, `RegistrationClosed`, `SessionStarted`, `SessionEnded`
